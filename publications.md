## Research Areas

My publications and research span several key areas:

- **Optimization algorithms** for software effort estimation using Support Vector Regression and Particle Swarm Optimization
- **Financial prediction and bankruptcy forecasting** using comprehensive income and accrual accounting data
- **Machine learning applications** in project management and predictive analytics
- **Data mining** and statistical methods for real-world problem solving
- **Search and recommendation systems** for large-scale applications
